# TexLab

TeXtra bundles TexLab 5.26.0 for supported platforms. TexLab is copyright its
contributors and is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. The accompanying `LICENSE.txt`
contains the license text. Corresponding source is available from the pinned
[TexLab v5.26.0 source tree](https://github.com/latex-lsp/texlab/tree/v5.26.0).

The executable in `win32-x64/texlab.exe` was extracted from the official
`texlab-x86_64-windows.zip` release artifact. Its SHA-256 is recorded in
`manifest.json`.
